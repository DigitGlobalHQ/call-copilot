How to use

  1. Laptop call audio on speakers, not headphones. The phone has to hear them.
  2. Phone flat on the desk between you and the laptop, screen up, this page open.
  3. Tap Listen before the call and say "testing, testing" so it learns which voice is you.
  4. If listening ever stops, tap Listen again. It carries on and keeps everything said so far.
  5. Tap the INTERVIEW / MEETING label at the top to switch mode.
  6. Questions from the other side get an answer card on top. Follow-ups get a new card.
  7. Labels wrong? Tap Swap, or tap a ME / THEM label in the transcript.
  8. Missed one? Answer now catches the last words being spoken and answers the latest thing asked. Type lets you ask privately.
  9. Every line in the transcript has an Answer button. Tap it to get an answer to that exact line, even from earlier in the call.
  10. Keep this screen open. Phones stop the mic when you switch apps or lock the screen.
